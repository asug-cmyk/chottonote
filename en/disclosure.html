Rankings and recommendations reflect the operator's own perspective; no manufacturer or retailer receives favorable treatment in exchange for placement.

Profile & Review Environment

I live in a rented apartment in the Tokyo area and work primarily at a desk at home, so I research these products from the same position as anyone else trying to decide what to buy. I'm especially interested in desk gadgets and storage items that don't damage walls or floors, and I always check whether an item works in a rental (does it require drilling, can it be removed without a trace) before recommending it.

When building a comparison article, I follow these rules:

  • Comparison tables and write-ups are based on each product's official specs and pricing, plus multiple buyer reviews from Amazon and Rakuten Ichiba
  • Rankings are never influenced by compensation or favors from manufacturers or retailers
  • Prices, stock, and specs change, so articles are reviewed periodically around their listed update date

Articles based on hands-on testing include a "How We Verified This" section in the body.
