What Is the Prime Appreciation Sale 2026? Dates and Deal Info

Amazon's Prime Appreciation Sale is a large sale for Prime members. In 2026 an early sale is followed by the main sale. Details come from Amazon's official announcement (updated 2026-09-24).

Early sale
Tue, Oct 13, 2026, 0:00 to Thu, Oct 15, 23:59 (JST)
Main sale
Fri, Oct 16, 2026, 0:00 to Mon, Oct 19, 23:59 (JST)

Deal Info (from Amazon's Announcement)

Who can join
The main sale is for Amazon Prime members only. The announcement says first-time Prime sign-ups get a 30-day free trial.
Number of items
Over 4 million items (over 2 million last year).
Point-up campaign
Entry is required (Thu, Sep 24, 14:00 to Mon, Oct 19, 23:59). The shopping period is Tue, Oct 13, 0:00 to Mon, Oct 19, 23:59. Spend a total of ¥10,000 or more (tax included) in that period to earn bonus points. The bonus is capped at 10,000 points (limited-time points). Some items, such as Apple products, are excluded. The announcement does not state the exact point rate.
Prize draw
Everyone who entered and spent a total of ¥10,000 or more (tax included) in the period is included, with no extra entry. Up to 100,000 points can be won.
Payment perks
Paying with an Amazon Mastercard earns extra points. Also, people linking d POINT to their Amazon account for the first time can get back up to 20% of their purchase (up to 1,000 points) as d POINT.
Daily deals
From Oct 16 to 19, a selection of items goes on sale each day from 0:00 to 23:59 (BRAUN, Dyson, Bose and others). Items change every day at noon and are announced on the day.
Latest conditions
Conditions can change, so check each campaign's detail page for the latest (the announcement says the same).

See Amazon's announcement (in Japanese) ↗

How to Get the Most Out of It

  1. Enter the point-up campaign first. Entry is one of the conditions for the bonus points.
  2. Make your total ¥10,000 or more (tax included). Every product below costs more than ¥10,000 on its own.
  3. Watch the price during the sale. Each product below shows its Amazon reference price as of 2026-10-09. A lower price during the sale is a good sign.
  4. Check the model number and the seller. Colors and older models often sit side by side. On the linked page check the model number and the seller (the seller and shipper) before you buy.
