🙂 Good points

  • Dehumidifies, humidifies (up to 400 mL/h) and purifies air (up to 21 tatami mats) in one unit
  • Plasmacluster 25000; dries 2 kg of laundry in about 165 minutes (maker's test value, 60 Hz)
  • A swing louver concentrates the airflow on the laundry

🙁 Things to note

  • The dehumidifying tray is about 2.0 L and dehumidifying stops when it is full; the maker advises care about once a week
  • At W350 × D285 × H656 mm and about 13 kg it is large, so check you have the space
