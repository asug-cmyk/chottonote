About the scoresThe scores are not from hands-on testing. They are calculated mechanically by applying the criteria table at the end of the page to figures published on the manufacturers' official pages and manuals (and on retail or comparison pages where an official figure was not available). We call it the "Spec Score". It does not reflect how a product feels to use.

Trouble falling asleep, a sore back or shoulders every morning, or moving into a new place and wanting a fresh mattress — whatever the reason, searching for a mattress quickly gets confusing. Firmness ratings and coil construction vary by brand, and prices range from around ¥12,000 to over ¥70,000, making it hard to know where to start. Mattresses mainly differ in three ways: sleep feel from the core material and firmness (how well pressure is distributed), durability from the thickness and construction (coil count, density, resistance to sagging), and how generous the warranty or trial period is (return and exchange terms). Here we compare four products with a Spec Score built from five items: warranty, trial period, thickness, coil count and extras.

Pros and Cons of Mattresses

Here's what tends to satisfy people after replacing their mattress, and what's worth checking before you buy.

Pros

  • Choosing a firmness that suits your body can make sleeping more comfortable (individual results vary)
  • A build that considers airflow and pressure distribution is designed with night sweats and ease of turning over in mind (individual results vary)
  • Many ship compressed and rolled, so you can unbox and set them up right at your door

Cons

  • Since you can't lie down and test it before buying, how firm it feels varies a lot by person
  • A new-mattress smell from the compressed packaging can linger, and some products need a few hours to a day of airing out after unboxing
  • Thicker models come in larger, heavier packaging, so check your delivery access beforehand

How to Choose a Mattress: 7 Points

Here are 7 points to check before you buy. The figures in the diagrams come from the official and retail-page specs of the products in this article.

1

How to Read Firmness (N Values)

Firmness is sometimes given in newtons (N); a bigger number means firmer. But makers measure different things (the foam alone or the whole mattress), so figures cannot be compared across brands directly. Here, GOKUMIN (urethane, 180N) and Koala (265N) publish figures on their official pages.

2

Know the Constructions

What is inside changes how it supports you, how it breathes and how heavy it is. Of the four here, three combine pocket coils with urethane, and Koala is urethane foam with no coils.

3

Check the Thickness

A thicker mattress tends to feel less like you are touching the floor beneath. It also ships heavier, so check your delivery route.

4

Look at the Coil Count (Coil Types)

With pocket coils, more coils tend to mean finer support. Products without coils (Koala) are left out. Figures are for the single size.

5

Check the Warranty Length

A warranty covers defects and problems that appear during use. Check the length, and what is covered, before you buy.

6

Trial Period (Can You Return It?)

Firmness is hard to judge without lying on it, so a trial period with returns gives peace of mind. Only products that state a trial period are shown.

7

Extras and Assurances

Whether each has an antibacterial finish, a safety certification, a washable cover and compressed packaging, in one table.

Commonly Cited Complaints, and How to Handle Them

Across mattresses in general, a point commonly raised is that a new-mattress smell from the compressed packaging is noticeable right after unboxing. Airing it out for a few hours to a day in a well-ventilated room usually reduces the smell significantly. Another commonly raised point is that the mattress feels firmer or softer than expected — an outcome that's hard to avoid given how much firmness perception varies by person. Checking the return/exchange terms and trial period in advance, or trying a similarly firm mattress in a physical store first if possible, can help reduce the chance of a mismatch. Thicker models can take longer to fully expand after being compressed, so even if it doesn't reach its full thickness right after unboxing, it's worth waiting about 24 hours before judging the feel.

Best Pick by Situation

If you want to balance sleep feel and durability between coils and foam, go with the Novilla Black Knight. If you want a firm, supportive feel, go with the GOKUMIN Premium Spring. If you value peace of mind through a trial period and warranty, go with the Koala Original. If you want to keep the cost down and just try one out, go with the ZINUS Classic Pocket Coil (DST).

First Steps After You Buy

Once you open the packaging, take the mattress out in a well-ventilated room, lay it flat, and let it sit for a few hours to a day. Compressed mattresses often haven't fully returned to their true thickness right after unboxing, and leaving them for about 24 hours brings them closer to their intended thickness and firmness. Odors can transfer from the packaging material, so airing it out with the windows open helps reduce any lingering smell. If you're using it on a bed frame, a slatted base or a breathable frame helps prevent moisture buildup and keeps the sleep feel more consistent. For products with a warranty or trial period, it's worth noting the terms (how to apply, the deadline) at the time of purchase so you're ready if you ever need them.

How the Spec Score Is Calculated

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, and the overall score is the weighted average. We use the manufacturer's official figures; where the official site could not be read, we fill in from Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Kakaku.com and similar retail or comparison pages (only pages that match the exact model number). A criterion with no published figure is left out and the average is taken over the rest (for care and features, only items that are actually listed earn points).
