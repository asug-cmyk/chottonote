Time-Saving Appliances for Living Alone: 6 Picks to Cut Down on Chores

Published: 2026-09-16 / Updated: 2026-09-16

When you live alone, the time you can spend on chores between work and everything else is limited. Bringing in appliances that cut down on the effort of daily chores frees up that time for other things. Here we've rounded up six appliance categories from our existing comparisons that help reduce chore time for people living alone. Each comparison article lines up multiple products against specific criteria, so check out the full articles if anything catches your eye.

1. Cordless Vacuum: Less setup and put-away time

Unlike a corded or upright vacuum that needs an outlet and takes effort to get out and put away, a cordless vacuum's main strength is being able to grab it and clean the moment you notice something. Suction power and runtime vary a lot between models, so it's worth matching your pick to your room size and how often you clean.

Read more: Cordless Vacuum Comparison, Choosing by Suction Power and Runtime

2. Electric Kettle: Less waiting for water to boil

Compared to a stovetop kettle, an electric kettle boils faster and works well for coffee, instant noodles, and simple cooking. Living alone, a compact 0.8L model is often plenty, and a model with temperature control lets you dial in the right heat for whatever you're making.

Read more: Electric Kettle Comparison, Choosing by Pour Control and Keep-Warm Function

3. Kitchen Trash Can: Less hassle sorting and tossing

How a lid opens and how easy it is to sort trash directly affects small daily frustrations. Switching to a pedal-operated lid or a can with built-in dividers for sorting makes the everyday act of throwing things away noticeably smoother.

Read more: Kitchen Trash Can Comparison, Choosing by Lid Mechanism and Sorting Ease

4. Dish Drying Rack: Faster dish cleanup

How much cleanup a dish rack takes afterward depends on its drainage type and setup style. Some models have a drain tray that's easy to rinse, while others sit over the sink to free up counter space — pick based on how much kitchen space you have.

Read more: Dish Drying Rack Comparison, Choosing by Drainage Type and Size

5. Humidifier: Less frequent refilling and upkeep

Humidifying method has a big effect on how often you refill the tank and how much upkeep is needed. A larger tank means fewer refills, and a model that's easy to clean keeps hygiene-related maintenance to a minimum.

Read more: Humidifier Comparison, Choosing by Humidifying Method and Ease of Cleaning

6. Air Purifier: Less frequent cleaning

Choosing a model with more coverage than your actual room size means the filter collects dust more slowly, cutting down on how often you need to clean or replace it. Stronger filtration also means the air gets cycled through more efficiently, which helps too.

Read more: Air Purifier Comparison, Choosing by Room Coverage and Filtration Performance

What to check when choosing time-saving appliances

Rather than picking on price alone, it helps to think about how much effort a given appliance will actually save in your own routine. Each comparison article lines up products by criteria like capacity, runtime, and ease of cleaning — match those against your room size and how often you'd use it.
