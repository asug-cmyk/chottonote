If you're waking up with a scratchy throat, or dealing with dry skin and static electricity, and start searching for a humidifier, you'll quickly run into products whose humidifying method and room-coverage figures are listed in inconsistent ways, making it hard to tell which one is actually right for you. Humidifiers mainly differ along three lines: humidifying method (evaporative, ultrasonic, heating, or a hybrid combining multiple methods), whether they have enough power for your room (does the stated room coverage match the room you'll actually use it in), and how easy they are to refill and clean (tank capacity, how wide the fill opening is, and whether the filter needs replacing). Here we compare four products on the five Spec Score items shown later in the article (humidifying power, care, quietness, energy use and extra features).

Pros and Cons of Humidifiers

Before you consider buying one, here's a rundown of the benefits of using a humidifier and the points worth keeping in mind.

Pros

  • Can help ease a scratchy throat, dry skin and static electricity (results vary from person to person)
  • You can choose the humidifying method and tank capacity to match your room size and lifestyle
  • Some models add extra features beyond humidifying, such as an air-purifying mode or aroma diffusing

Cons

  • If you're already using a humidifier that suits your room size and have no complaints, there's little need to replace it
  • If you only want to humidify a small area — like your desk at the office or right by your pillow — a smaller tabletop humidifier is easier to work with
  • If your lifestyle doesn't call for using a humidifier much in the first place, this isn't a high priority

How to Choose a Humidifier: 6 Points

Here are 6 points to check before you buy. The figures in the diagrams come from the official and retail-page specs of the products in this article.

1

Match the Rated Room Size to Your Room

If output is too low, the air stays dry. If it is too high, you invite condensation and mold. Start by comparing the size of your room with each product's rated room coverage.

2

Know the Humidifying Methods

The method changes noise, upkeep and how quickly it gets going. All the products in this article are hybrid types (they combine heating with another method).

3

Ease of Cleaning

A humidifier handles water, so how much upkeep it needs decides whether you keep using it. We look at four things.

4

Operating Noise (Especially for a Bedroom)

If it will sit in a bedroom, check the noise of its quietest mode.

5

Power Consumption (A Guide to Running Costs)

Methods that use heat draw more power. If running costs matter, look at the power used in each mode.

6

Extra Features

Auto mode, timer, remote and other handy extras, in one table.

Who Humidifiers Are a Good Fit For — and Who Should Consider Other Options

As a category, humidifiers tend to work well for the following people.

Good fit if you...

  • Want to ease a scratchy throat, dry skin or static (results vary from person to person)
  • Want humidifying output matched to the size of the room
  • Also want extra functions such as an air-purifying mode

Consider other options if you...

  • Already have a humidifier sized to your room and are happy with it
  • Only want to humidify a very small area, such as beside a pillow (a smaller desktop humidifier suits better)
  • Do not really use a humidifier as part of your routine

Common Concerns, and How to Handle Them

The points below are general ones often made about humidifiers; we have not used these four products to confirm them. Mineral scale and slime in the water tank, and mold or odor in the filter or tray, are common concerns with humidifiers. Sharp and Dainichi both advise washing the filter and tray about once every two weeks (on their official pages). Always check each product's manual for its own cleaning schedule. Ultrasonic and hybrid models are generally said to leave a white mineral residue from tap water on nearby surfaces, so if you place one near flooring or furniture, laying down a mat is a good precaution. For evaporative and hybrid filters, drying them thoroughly before off-season storage is generally said to help prevent odors the next time you use it.

First Steps After You Buy

Before you start using it, check the manual's setup and placement instructions. Placement requirements (distance from walls and furniture, height off the floor and so on) differ by product, so follow the manual. It also helps to note the cleaning schedule (Sharp and Dainichi officially advise washing about every two weeks) and the filter replacement timing, so nothing catches you off guard mid-season.

How the Spec Score Is Calculated

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, and the overall score is the weighted average. We use the manufacturer's official figures; where the official site could not be read, we fill in from Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Kakaku.com and similar retail or comparison pages (only pages that match the exact model number). A criterion with no published figure is left out and the average is taken over the rest (for care and features, only items that are actually listed earn points).
