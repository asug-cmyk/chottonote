Care Notes

These care points come from the sources. The Pearl Metal HC-513 has an epoxy-coated steel basket, and its retail page says not to use it in a dishwasher because it can deform. The Latuna Roll-Up Rack can go in a dishwasher, though it may not fit in some dishwashers. In general, wiping off excess water after use helps any material last longer. For over-the-sink types, measuring the inside of your sink before buying makes it easier to judge whether it will fit.

First Steps After You Buy

After setup, try actually washing dishes and note where water tends to collect; that makes it easier to adjust the placement or angle. For over-the-sink types, it is worth checking once after installation that the fit is right and there is no wobbling. If water does collect, emptying it soon after you finish washing helps keep limescale and mold from building up.

How the Spec Score Is Calculated

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, and the overall score is the weighted average. We use the manufacturer's official figures; where the official site could not be read, we fill in from Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Kakaku.com and similar retail or comparison pages (only pages that match the exact model number). A criterion with no published figure is left out and the average is taken over the rest (for care and features, only items that are actually listed earn points).
