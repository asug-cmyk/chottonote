Pros and Cons of Entryway Storage Benches

Here's a summary of what tends to satisfy people after buying, and what's worth checking before you do.

Pros

  • You can sit down to put your shoes on, making entryway routines easier
  • Tiers or drawers let you store more shoes
  • A product that shows a weight capacity figure gives you a rough guide for sitting

Cons

  • Without enough depth and width in your entryway, it can be hard to find space to place one
  • Many products take some effort to assemble yourself from multiple parts
  • If you don't need extra storage, a regular chair may be enough

How to Choose an Entryway Storage Bench: 6 Points

Here are 6 points to check before you buy. The figures in the diagrams come from the official and retail-page specs of the products in this article.

1

Compare the Weight Capacity

When you put on shoes, your whole weight lands on the seat at once. If you will sit on it, compare the weight capacity figures. A figure for the shelf alone is not a number for sitting. Some pages do not say the figure is for the seat, so check the retail page before buying.

2

Will It Fit? Measure the Depth

Entryways are shallower than they look. Measure the depth of the spot first, and make sure the bench will not block the door or the way through.

3

Width, Shelves and Drawers Set the Storage

How many shoes fit depends mostly on the width and the number of shelves. If you want somewhere for small items, look for a drawer. A wider bench also needs a wider spot.

4

Know the Materials

The material changes weight, resistance to humidity and upkeep. These four use bamboo, wood board, steel, and resin with a metal frame.

5

Seat and Leg Features

You sit here every day, so look at the cushion, how easy the seat or body is to clean, and whether the legs protect the floor. Here they are in one table.

6

Assembly and Weight

Many products in this category are assembled at home, and a heavier one is harder to build and to place. After opening the box, check that all parts and screws are there, and build in the order of the instructions.

Who Entryway Storage Benches Are a Good Fit For — and Who Should Consider Other Options

As a category, entryway storage benches tend to work well for the following people.

Good fit if you...

  • Want somewhere to sit down to put shoes on in the entryway
  • Want more shoe storage, through tiers or drawers
  • Want to check the weight capacity figure before sitting down

Consider other options if you...

  • Do not have the entryway space to give up the width or the depth
  • Do not want to spend time assembling something from several parts
  • Already have a chair or other storage and do not really need to replace it

What to Look at When Choosing

This page scores five items: weight capacity, storage, how easily the bench fits, weight, and seat and leg features (weights and thresholds are in the criteria table at the end). Measure the depth of your spot first and decide whether you will sit on it; that narrows the candidates quickly.

Things to Watch During Assembly

Many products in this category are assembled at home. After opening the box, start by checking that all parts and screws are included, and assemble in the order the instructions specify without forcing anything. Bamboo and wooden products are generally said to be able to develop mold in humid seasons, so if your entryway runs humid, periodic wiping down is a good precaution. If the only rating shown is for a shelf, that is not a figure for sitting, so check the retail page before sitting on it.

First Steps After You Buy

After assembly, it is a good idea to double-check that every screw is fully tightened before actually sitting down. If you notice wobbling once you start using it, re-tightening the screws often fixes it. Bamboo or wooden benches can generally develop mold or warp from humidity, so airing out the room from the start helps you avoid that.

How the Spec Score Is Calculated

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, and the overall score is the weighted average. We use the manufacturer's official figures; where the official site could not be read, we fill in from Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Kakaku.com and similar retail or comparison pages (only pages that match the exact model number). A criterion with no published figure is left out and the average is taken over the rest (for care and features, only items that are actually listed earn points).
