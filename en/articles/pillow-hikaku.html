Pillow Comparison: 4 Models Ranked by Spec Score

Published: 2026-09-25 / Updated: 2026-10-07

We scored four pillows on height adjustment, washability, sleeping position, heat and size, using only published specs from the makers (some from retail pages).

About the scoresThe scores are not from hands-on testing. They are calculated mechanically by applying the criteria table at the end of the page to figures published on the manufacturers' official pages and manuals (and on retail or comparison pages where an official figure was not available). We call it the "Spec Score". It does not reflect how a product feels to use.

Shopping for a pillow gets confusing fast. The fills are completely different from one another — memory foam, polyethylene pipe, layered foam — and prices run from around ¥5,000 to nearly ¥30,000. But one thing people tend to regret about a pillow is not the material but the height. And the right height for you is honestly not something you can work out before you buy, even after lying on one in a shop for a few minutes. So this comparison uses two axes instead: can you change the height after you own it, and how much of the pillow can you actually wash. All four adjust — but they adjust in completely different ways.

Upsides and Downsides of Replacing Your Pillow

What tends to make people glad they switched, and what to check before you order.

Upsides

  • Cheaper than a mattress, and it still changes your sleeping posture — so getting it wrong costs little
  • An adjustable pillow can be re-set later, when the season changes or a new mattress sinks differently
  • A washable pillow can be cleaned before sweat and odour build up in it

Downsides

  • The right height depends on your build and how far your mattress sinks, so other people's "too high" or "too low" impressions may not transfer to you
  • Among these four, only the Kata-Rakune can have the pillow body washed; for the other three only the cover can (for the AiR 4D, per a retail page)
  • On some pillows lowering the height is just a matter of removing fill, but raising it needs the supplied refill pack (Kata-Rakune) or sheets (AiR 4D)

How to Choose a Pillow: 6 Points

Here are 6 points to check before you buy. The figures in the diagrams come from the official and retail-page specs of the products in this article.

1

Know How the Height Is Adjusted

Height is one of the things people tend to regret about a pillow, and you cannot know the right height before you buy. So look at whether you can change it afterwards, and how.

2

How Much Can Be Washed

A pillow takes sweat every night. Check whether only the cover washes or the pillow body does too. Here we count only what the maker states can be washed. For the AiR 4D only a retail page mentions washing, so it gets 1 point here.

3

Does It Suit Your Sleeping Position

The height you need changes between back, side and front sleeping. See whether the maker states support for a position, or how to set it up.

4

Size (Room to Turn Over)

Width gives a rough idea of whether your head stays on the pillow when you turn over. For a larger pillow, also check the space and the pillowcase size.

5

Measures Against Heat and Stuffiness

The head traps heat. Look for breathable fill or structure, a layer that carries off heat, and a breathable cover or sides.

6

Pairing With Your Mattress

How a pillow's height feels depends not only on its own number but also on how it pairs with your mattress (the points below are general guides and vary from person to person). If you are considering replacing both, see Mattress Comparison: Choosing by Sleep Feel and Durability as well.

Height-adjustable pillows as a category tend to suit the following people.

  • Have just changed mattress and found your pillow no longer works
  • Sweat at night and care whether the pillow can be washed

Consider other options if you

  • Are happy with the height and feel of the pillow you have
  • Are set on a particular fill, such as buckwheat husk or beads
  • Have ongoing neck or shoulder pain in the morning and are not sure the pillow is the cause — seeing a doctor first is the more reliable route

What Tends to Catch People Out After Buying, and What to Do

What tends to happen with any pillow is that it turns out higher or lower than expected. That is less a fault of the product than a reflection of the fact that the right height shifts with your build and how far your mattress sinks — which is why someone else's impression does not transfer to you. It is also why this comparison weighs how far the height can be changed later rather than the height itself. In general, a new foam pillow is said to hold a smell when first opened; if so, follow the care label and air it somewhere well ventilated. Another point that is easy to miss until after purchase is that the cover washes but the pillow does not. The washability table above and each product page follow each maker's official wording (for the AiR 4D alone, where the maker says nothing about washing, a retail page, so it gets 1 point); where a maker does not say something is washable, we have not said it either.

Which to Pick, by Situation

If you want both a washable pillow and fine height control, the Nishikawa Kata-Rakune Health Pillow. If you want to find your height first without spending much, the GOKUMIN Premium Memory Foam Pillow. If you would rather decide by sleeping position, or want the longest warranty, the Emma Pillow Premium. If you need to pad the neck or one side on its own, the Nishikawa AiR 4D Pillow.

What to Do First After It Arrives

When it arrives, start by reading the care label and the included instructions. For a height-adjustable pillow, it is generally said to be easier to find the right position by starting low and adjusting a little at a time (the GOKUMIN is chosen from four fixed settings rather than built up). As a general guide, look for a height where, lying on your back, there is no awkward gap behind your neck and your chin does not tilt upward. Then roll onto your side — if your shoulder compresses and your head drops, add height where the pillow allows it. Give each setting a few nights before judging it, rather than deciding after one (how it feels varies from person to person). If you bought a washable pillow, read the care label first and note what can be washed and how to dry it. For how to dry fill such as pipe, follow the product's care label.

How the Spec Score Is Calculated

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, and the overall score is the weighted average. We use the manufacturer's official figures; where the official site could not be read, we fill in from Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Kakaku.com and similar retail or comparison pages (only pages that match the exact model number). A criterion with no published figure is left out and the average is taken over the rest (for care and features, only items that are actually listed earn points).

Summary

This comparison uses published specs, not how the pillows felt to sleep on. Since the right height is hard to know before you buy, weigh whether it can be changed afterwards and how much can be washed, then pick by your sleeping position and budget.
