Pillow Comparison: Choosing by Height Adjustment and Washability

Published: 2026-09-25 / Updated: 2026-09-25

With a pillow, how you adjust the height and how much of it you can wash decide almost everything about living with it.

Shopping for a pillow gets confusing fast. The fills are completely different from one another — memory foam, polyethylene pipe, layered foam — and prices run from around ¥5,000 to nearly ¥30,000. But the thing people most often regret about a pillow is not the material. It is the height. And the right height for you is honestly not something you can work out before you buy, even after lying on one in a shop for a few minutes. So this comparison uses two axes instead: can you change the height after you own it, and how much of the pillow can you actually wash. All four adjust — but they adjust in completely different ways.

Upsides and Downsides of Replacing Your Pillow

What tends to make people glad they switched, and what to check before you order.

Upsides

  • Cheaper than a mattress, and it still changes your sleeping posture — so getting it wrong costs little
  • An adjustable pillow can be re-set later, when the season changes or a new mattress sinks differently
  • A washable pillow can be cleaned before sweat and odour build up in it

Downsides

  • The right height depends on your build and how far your mattress sinks, so other people's "too high" or "too low" reviews do not transfer to you
  • With foam pillows, the pillow itself often cannot be washed. Frequently, only the cover can
  • Lowering the height is just a matter of removing fill, but raising it needs extra fill, which not every pillow supplies

What to Look at When Choosing

We compared on these three points.

1. How the height is adjusted (fixed steps, 1cm increments, or section by section)

2. How much can be washed (the pillow itself, or only the cover)

3. What the fill is (pipe, memory foam, or layered foam)

When you compare heights, the pillow's own number matters less than how it pairs with your mattress. A soft mattress lets your shoulder sink, so you need less pillow; on a firm one, the same pillow feels too low. If you are considering replacing both, see Mattress Comparison: Choosing by Sleep Feel and Durability as well.

Who This Suits, and Who Should Look Elsewhere

Height-adjustable pillows as a category tend to suit the following people.

A good fit if you

  • Have replaced pillow after pillow without ever finding the right height
  • Have just changed mattress and found your pillow no longer works
  • Sweat at night and care whether the pillow can be washed

Consider other options if you

  • Are happy with the height and feel of the pillow you have
  • Are set on a particular fill, such as buckwheat husk or beads
  • Have ongoing neck or shoulder pain in the morning and are not sure the pillow is the cause — seeing a doctor first is the more reliable route

Common Complaints in Reviews, and What to Do About Them

The most common comment about any pillow is that it turned out higher or lower than expected. That is less a fault of the product than a reflection of the fact that the right height shifts with your build and how far your mattress sinks — which is exactly why someone else's review does not transfer to you. It is also why this comparison ranks pillows by how far the height can be changed later rather than by the height itself. The second common complaint is the smell of a new foam pillow, which settles down after a few hours to a day airing in a well-ventilated room out of direct sunlight. The third is discovering after purchase that the cover washes but the pillow does not. The "What can be washed" column above follows each maker's official wording exactly; where a maker does not say something is washable, we have not said it either.

Which to Pick, by Situation

If you want both a washable pillow and fine height control, the Nishikawa Kata-Rakune Health Pillow. If you want to find your height first without spending much, the GOKUMIN Premium Memory Foam Pillow. If you would rather decide by sleeping position, or want the longest warranty, the Emma Pillow Premium. If you need to pad the neck or one side on its own, the Nishikawa AiR 4D Pillow.

What to Do First After It Arrives

When it arrives, start from the lowest setting. It is much easier to find the right position by adding height from below than by taking it away from above. As a guide: lying on your back, there should be no awkward gap behind your neck and your chin should not tilt upward. Then roll onto your side — if your shoulder compresses and your head drops, add height. Give each setting two or three nights before judging it, rather than deciding after one. Foam pillows can hold a smell when new, so airing one for half a day before use makes a noticeable difference. If you bought a washable pillow, read the care label first and note what can be washed and how to dry it (pipe fill needs to be dried thoroughly out of direct sun) — it saves guessing later.
